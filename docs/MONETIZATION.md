# Monetization Philosophy (ZIRA V1)

ZIRA V1 is completely free to end users:
- ❌ No paywalls or subscriptions
- ❌ No mid-roll video interruptions
- ❌ No pop-unders or fake download buttons

Revenue is generated cleanly through:
1. **Direct Sponsor Banners**: Managed corporate sponsor partnerships.
2. **Affiliate Placements**: Partner campaigns promoting smart TVs, internet packages, and devices.
3. **House Ads**: Internal promotions for new original releases and the ZIRA PWA.
